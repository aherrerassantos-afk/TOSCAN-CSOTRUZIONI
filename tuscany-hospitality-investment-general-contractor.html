Tuscany Hospitality Investment & Turnkey General Contractor: Hotels, PBSA & Short-Medium Term Assets

Quick Answer (Executive Summary): Toscan Costruzioni (Part of Blue Arroyo Group, headquartered in Florence, Italy) is Tuscany's vertically integrated Hospitality General Contractor specializing in the turnkey creation of Short & Medium Term rental buildings (Specializzazione), Boutique Hotels, Agriturismi & Wine Resorts, and Student Housing (PBSA) across Florence, Siena, Chianti, Val d'Orcia, Pisa, and Lucca.

We eliminate construction risk for foreign investors through fixed-price Costo chiavi in mano contracts (€1,350 – €2,450/sqm including FF&E) and penalty-backed timelines (6 – 12 months) across two business models: (1) Closed-End Per-Transaction appalto chiavi in mano (Contratto a Corpo appalto direttos, prezzo bloccato e tempi garantiti con penalitarget valore dell'immobile) and (2) Single Investor Chiavi in Mano.

Two Investment & Execution Models for Foreign Capital

Model 01 · Closed-End Contratto a Corpo appalto diretto

Per-Transaction appalto chiavi in mano (Co-Investment)

Co-invest alongside Blue Arroyo Group in a dedicated Italian Special Purpose Vehicle (Contratto a Corpo) holding a single high-yield hospitality or short/medium-term asset in Tuscany. Tickets from prezzo fisso a contratto to €500,000+, with construction costs locked upfront by Toscan Costruzioni to protect investor equity and deliver a prezzo bloccato e tempi garantiti con penalitarget valore dell'immobile.

Model 02 · Single Investor Chiavi in Mano

Vision-to-Keys General Contracting

Acquiring a historic palazzo in Florence, a stone estate in Chianti, or a coastal hotel in Versilia? Appoint Toscan Costruzioni as your sole English-speaking Design & Build General Contractor. We manage architectural zoning, Superintendency (Fine Arts) approvals, structural seismic upgrades, MEP, acoustic insulation, and contract FF&E under one fixed-price contract.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does Toscan Costruzioni protect international investors from Italian bureaucracy and cost overruns?

We issue a 48-hour pre-acquisition Technical & Financial Feasibility Study, handle all municipal and Soprintendenza permits in-house, and sign a lump-sum Chiavi in Mano contract with weekly English video/photo SAL progress reports and delay penalties.

Can we invest remotely without residing in Italy?

Yes. Both our Contratto a Corpo appalto direttos (including fractional luxury ownership such as Villa Claudia in Chianti DOCG at €298,000 per 1/8 share) and our Single Investor Chiavi in Mano contracts can be executed via power of attorney and digital reporting.
